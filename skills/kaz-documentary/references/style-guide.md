# Kaz Documentary style guide

Distilled from four reference edits: a map evidence-board film, a news-on-paper piece, a scrapbook dossier and a crime-scene board. The vintage 2D layer was added afterwards.

## 1. Grammar (always)
- **One continuous camera** over one big world surface. Pushes, pulls, pans and slight tilts; no hard cuts.
- **Shallow 3D cards:** rotated ±2–8°, soft drop shadow, cream paper border.
- **One accent colour (red #d0312d).** Use it for circles, underlines, strings, pins, stamps and key labels, and for nothing else.
- **Marks synced to the voiceover:** a circle or underline draws on the spoken word in 8–15 frames, with a hand-drawn wobble.
- **Two typefaces:** a typewriter mono for labels and the "old idea", a bold serif for years, names and the thesis.
- **Texture:** grain, vignette, slight flicker.

## 2. Beat recipes
REVEAL_PHOTO (slide in, back-out settle, slow push) · DOC_FOCUS (whip to a document, circle the key phrase) · YEAR_CARD (dim, warm flash, serif year rolls between rules) · NAME_TAG (black-tape mono label types on, red leader line to a pin) · CONNECT (red string draws A→B) · STAMP (FAILED/DENIED slams on with overshoot, shake and RGB split; one or two per video) · HIGHLIGHT_LINE (red brush swipes words) · THESIS (mono line, red strike, serif answer, red full stop) · NETWORK_REVEAL (pull out to every item) · COUNTER · TITLE_GLITCH (red serif title, glitch slices, fade to black).

Pacing: holds of 3–5 seconds early on, faster toward the climax. Tags fade about 7 seconds after appearing, then all of them return for the network reveal.

## 3. Surfaces
| Surface | Look | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Map Board | Dark teal, tinted map, pins and strings | Events across places |
| Paper Press | Cream grid paper, highlighter, cut-outs | News and politics |
| Scrapbook Dossier | Charcoal, polaroids, masking tape, typewriter | Biographies |
| Chalk Case Board | Green-black top-down, white tape, chalk notes | Crime and conspiracies |
| Digital Coda | Black, counters, particles | The modern turn |

## 4. Vintage 2D inserts (silent-film)
Use for the dramatic action beat (an attack, a chase, a turning point) where no photo exists.
- **Palette:** sepia paper (226,208,172), ink (52,38,26); red only for the route, target circles, the subject label and the intertitle accents.
- **Animate on twos** (12 fps steps) with boiling lines (re-jitter outlines each step). Switch to full 30 fps only for the impact moment.
- **Film treatment:** sepia grade, grain σ≈13, flicker ±7%, 0–2 vertical scratches, dust specks, gate-weave jitter and a heavy vignette.
- **Structure:** iris in → schematic map with the red dashed route and the event circled → iris → side-view scene with flat silhouettes, the subject stepping out of the crowd and a white flash with a frame jump on impact, then smoke → **intertitle card** (black, double border, red corner dots, serif text) → iris out to the board.
- **Sound:** projector clatter at 24/s, hiss, an engine putter, board SFX halved.
- Keep it non-graphic.

## 5. Tokens
Teal background BGR (42,40,22); map tint dark (40,38,20) to light (150,150,112) in BGR; cream (234,227,210); red (208,49,45) RGB. Fonts: FreeMono Bold for mono, Caladea Bold for serif (check that glyphs render; GFS Baskerville has no Latin capitals).
